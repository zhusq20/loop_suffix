Spec-Bench was introduced by Xia et al.~\cite{xia2024unlocking}
as a benchmark for evaluating speculative decoding across multi-turn
conversation, translation, summarization, question answering,
mathematical reasoning, and retrieval-augmented generation.
Its 480 examples include 80 two-turn MT-Bench conversations and
400 single-turn examples. Our experiments use the first turn of
each example.

The decode-latency experiments use batch size 1 and a maximum of
512 newly generated tokens per request. The throughput experiments
use all 480 SpecBench prompts at batch sizes
$B\in\{1,2,4,8,16\}$, with a maximum of 1,024 newly generated tokens
per request. These limits exclude prompt tokens; generation stops
earlier upon an end-of-sequence or end-of-turn token.
